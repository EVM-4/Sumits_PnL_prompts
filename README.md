The story begins with an options trader frustrated at the lack of a proper application to track the profit and losses on options traded. 
Unwilling to give up on the prospect of having a marketable product to track option profit and losses, he turns to his children to realize his vision.
The children however are unprepaed to even embark on such a task, As such the father seeks the help of his good friend, a coding master, to train the children.
The master understands the plight of the trader as well as the incompetence of the children. Yet, he takes it upon himself to do what he can.
He gives the children the vision - to build the ultmate options Profit and Loss tool - but reminds them they have a long way to go.
To build their capabilities as coders, he gives them tasks, each harder than the last. The now students are ready to begin their journy.

This repository consists of all tasks that one particular student has completed to reach the end goal....
